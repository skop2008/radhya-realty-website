4. Data Principal Statutory Rights (Access, Correction & Erasure)

Under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, clients (Data Principals) possess irrevocable statutory rights:

  • Right to Access & Summary: You may request a verified summary of personal data held in our CRM vault and the processing actions undertaken.
  • Right to Correction & Updating: You may update or correct incomplete, inaccurate, or outdated contact or KYC records at any time.
  • Right to Withdraw Consent & Permanent Erasure (Right to be Forgotten): You may withdraw your consent for advisory communication. Upon receipt of consent withdrawal, Radhya Realty issues a 48-hour pre-erasure confirmation notice and permanently purges all stored client documents from our cryptographic vault, subject only to statutory tax and financial record-keeping mandates.
  • Right of Nomination: You have the right to nominate an individual to exercise your data principal rights in the event of death or incapacity.
